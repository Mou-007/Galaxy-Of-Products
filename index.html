Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Multiplatform Publisher is a personal content scheduling and publishing application. This policy explains how information is handled when the application connects to social media services.

2. Information We Process

The application may process:

  • Social media access tokens and account identifiers.
  • Post captions, titles, images, and destination links.
  • Scheduled publishing dates and times.
  • Publishing statuses, platform post IDs, and error logs.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to manage scheduled content, publish posts to social media accounts authorized by the account owner, and monitor publishing results.

4. Storage and Third-Party Services

Application records and configuration are stored in the application environment. Media images may be uploaded to Cloudinary for hosting and delivery.

When publishing content, the application communicates with the relevant social media platforms, including Meta services and Pinterest, according to the permissions granted by the account owner.

5. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold or rented. Information is transmitted only as necessary to provide the application's publishing and media-hosting functionality.

6. Data Security

Access credentials are maintained in the application's configuration environment. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information and restrict unauthorized access.

7. Data Deletion and Revoking Access

The account owner can delete application records and revoke the application's access through the relevant social media platform settings. Uploaded media may also need to be deleted from the media hosting service.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the contact details associated with the application.
